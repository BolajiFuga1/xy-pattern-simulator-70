Z1–Z16 and the rules

Rules used

  • Step 1: each draw picks 5 different numbers from 1–70 and plots them as one line across columns 1–70. Draw again (up to max draws) until a line of five repeats, then print the two lines. If no line repeats, print NONE and stop everything.
  • Step 2: pick 4 different patterns from Z1–Z16 as C1–C4, shown as C4 C3 C2 C1.
  • C5–C8: read across rows: row 1 of C1–C4 becomes C5, row 2 becomes C6, row 3 becomes C7, row 4 becomes C8.
  • Addition row by row: Y+Y=Y, Y+X=X, X+X=Y.
  • C9=C1+C2, C10=C3+C4, C11=C5+C6, C12=C7+C8, C13=C9+C10, C14=C11+C12, C15=C13+C14, C16=C15+C1.
  • If C15 is Z1, Z7 or Z11, pick 4 new patterns.
  • C17=C1+C4+C7+C8 and C20=C3+C7+C11+C15. C20 is Nil unless it is Z1, Z3, Z5, Z6, Z8, Z10, Z13 or Z15.
  • If 8 or more of C1–C16 equal C17, print them in a table and stop. Otherwise print None and do the next run.
